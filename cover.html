Garrison Resources for Soldiers

Training Game to better utilize Resources • Train the Force • Win the War

Mission Briefing

Description: In garrison, soldiers often take their problems (financial stress, family issues, mental fatigue, spiritual crisis) to the UMT because of absolute confidentiality. The UMT acts as a diagnostic filter. To successfully resolve a soldier's issue and restore their "Readiness Point," the UMT must route or collaborate with other garrison assets. GAME INSTRUCTIONS: Read Soldier's issue and pick one or two or three resources available for that particular issue including your UMT.
